Each valid calculation is automatically saved with the device time, BG, table, prior rate, recommended action, and rate change. Every press of Calculate creates a separate event, even if inputs are the same or checks are less than an hour apart. Recommendations do not establish administration.

Calculated events are saved automatically on this browser until cleared. Export before changing device, clearing browser data, or starting another patient. Do not use as an EHR record.
